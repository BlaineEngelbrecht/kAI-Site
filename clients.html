01 · Beauty & grooming

Xclusive Barber

WhatsApp booking automation · Durban

The challenge

Appointments were arranged by hand through calls and back and forth messages, which cost time during busy hours and let bookings slip through the cracks.

What kAI built

A WhatsApp booking bot that lets customers choose a service and a time slot and confirms the appointment automatically.

ResultFirst live kAI deployment

Running on a monthly retainer since launch in June 2026.

“Before kAI would get so many complaints, but now our customer service is top notch.”
02 · ICT & connectivity

Telekoms Wireless

Branded WhatsApp assistant · Durban

The challenge

Business enquiries came in around the clock, and every lead needed a fast, informed reply from someone who knew the products.

What kAI built

“Telekoms Wireless kAI”, a branded WhatsApp assistant trained on a structured knowledge base. It answers enquiries, qualifies leads and hands serious prospects to the team when a person is needed.

ResultEvery enquiry gets an instant response

The team can focus its time on qualified leads while the assistant handles the repeat questions.

03 · Education

Gateway City College

Student enquiry assistant · Durban

The challenge

Prospective and current students ask a high volume of similar questions, and staff cannot answer them all at once.

What kAI built

An AI assistant that handles student enquiries around the clock, so common questions are answered immediately and staff can focus on the cases that need them.

ResultEnquiries handled at scale

A dedicated kAI system for the college's student conversations.

04 · Food & events

Damascus Coffee

QR code ordering system · Durban

The challenge

Mobile and pop up vendors face long queues, rushed manual orders and mistakes at busy events.

What kAI built

A QR code ordering system where customers scan, browse the menu, order and pay from their phone, then receive an SMS when their order is ready.

ResultA reusable ordering product

First deployed at a campus event and designed to work for mobile and pop up vendors.
