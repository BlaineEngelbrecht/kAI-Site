Who We Are

Built around the reality of small business.

kAI Automation Industries builds AI chatbots and automation for South African SMEs.

We kept seeing the same thing. Owners spend their evenings answering the same five WhatsApp questions. Promising leads go cold overnight because no one is there to reply. Hours meant for growing the business disappear into admin.

We founded kAI to change that. We do not sell software you have to learn. We build and manage the systems for you, working quietly in the background, around the clock, so you can focus on what you do best.

We have deployed working automations across education, beauty, salons, and coaching, proving client by client that AI automation is not a future luxury. It is a practical tool any serious business can use today.

Small businesses are not a second market. They are our first. They deserve tools built for them, priced for them, and supported by people who understand their reality.
