Why kAI exists

An African company, for African business.

kAI Automation Industries began with a simple, frustrating observation: everyone was talking about AI, but almost no one was helping small businesses actually use it.

Over 98% of registered businesses in South Africa are SMEs. Big corporations had entire departments dedicated to automation. Meanwhile, the local salon owner, the independent real estate agent, and the one person coaching practice, the backbone of the African economy, were left to figure it out alone, or simply left behind.
